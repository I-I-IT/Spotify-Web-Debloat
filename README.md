#Spotify Debloat 

Just a way to have a cleaner, less distracting Spotify. Note that this doesn'tt and wouldn't remove what you might think is "useless" fucntionallity. If you want a minimalist ipod-like interface, other projects will fit you better.

This mainly removes distraction when playing songs (Canva), as well as the over promotion of podcasts. You can still play podcasts, but they will show less on the homepage. Also removes "get the app" nudges.
